## Results

### Quantitative

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Time spent on EOD reports | 2h/day | 15 min/day | **87% reduction** |
| Food waste | 15% | 8% | **47% reduction** |
| Order-to-kitchen time | 5 min | 30 sec | **90% faster** |
| Online orders | 0/month | 450/month | **New revenue stream** |
| FURS compliance errors | 3-5/month | 0/month | **100% compliant** |
| Monthly cost (old POS) | €165/month | €49/month | **70% savings** |

### ROI Calculation

```
Old POS: €165/month × 12 = €1,980/year
RestaurantOS: €49/month × 12 = €588/year
Annual savings: €1,392/year

Time saved: 1h45min/day × 365 = 641 hours/year
@ €15/hour = €9,615/year in labor savings

Food waste reduction: 7% × €50,000/month food cost = €3,500/month = €42,000/year

Total annual benefit: €1,392 + €9,615 + €42,000 = €53,007/year
RestaurantOS cost: €588/year
ROI: 89x (8,900%)
```
